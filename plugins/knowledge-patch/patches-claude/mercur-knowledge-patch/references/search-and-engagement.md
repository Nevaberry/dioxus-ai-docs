# Search and Engagement

## Search integration (0.9.0 and 1.0.0)

Algolia integration handles enhanced product data and product upserts and
updates.

Modifying inventory items triggers an Algolia update. Indexed product
availability can therefore follow inventory changes without a separate
product edit.

## Marketplace engagement (0.9.0)

Mercur includes:

- wishlists;
- seller and product reviews;
- Resend email integration; and
- a TalkJS conversation endpoint.

## Vendor-panel notifications (1.0.0)

The Vendor panel includes UI notifications, giving sellers an in-dashboard
channel for marketplace events.

Admin notifications are excluded from the seller feed, keeping seller-facing
notifications separate from administrator events.
